# Corrections and version history

Jeffery Kline is responsible for the mathematical claims and their correction.
Report a suspected error through a repository issue, identifying the version,
statement or file, and a witness or explanation. No email route is used here.

Material corrections receive a dated explanation and a new version. The
original public tag and archived bytes remain unchanged. A withdrawn claim
will remain identifiable with its reason for withdrawal and any replacement.
Later prior-art findings will narrow the contribution claim and credit the
earlier source; they will not be hidden by replacing an old archive.
